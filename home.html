Quant · MQL5 · Python

QuadBreak

An algorithmic trading system for gold (XAUUSD), written in MQL5. It runs a portfolio of independent strategies under volatility-targeted risk management, all researched on a tick-level backtesting engine I built in Python.

Profitable in every out-of-sample period (2025, Jul–Nov 2026), averaging ~40%/month with a Sharpe ratio of 3.1 and ~49% max drawdown in simulated backtests. Stress-tested across 12 cost & latency conditions, with lookahead checks and independent re-implementations. Now forward-testing on a live account.
Quant · MQL5 · Python

Alfred

A standalone intraday trading system for gold (XAUUSD), written in MQL5. It takes at most one trade a day, with an automatic stop, a profit lock, an end-of-day exit and capped loss-recovery sizing, and runs alongside QuadBreak as a diversifying second system. It was researched on the same tick-level engine and test pipeline.

Backtest (Jan 2025 – Nov 2026, compounding): 8.4%/month with a 19.1% max drawdown, $1,000 → $6,114, profitable in every period. Settings: 1% of balance per trade (×0.25 on quiet days), recovery sizing ×1.5 up to 2 steps, profit lock at +3R. Confirmed in the MT5 Strategy Tester.
